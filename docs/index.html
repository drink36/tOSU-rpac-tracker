RecSports staff enter these head counts. A reading only counts when its number had been updated shortly before it was recorded, so closed hours and counts left over from the night before are skipped. Percent means people ÷ posted capacity. Dashed cells and bars have fewer than 5 readings, and "mostly 0" means nobody was counted in most readings, which can mean the area is closed then.
